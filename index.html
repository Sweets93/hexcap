Bots play the real step() — there is no second simulation to drift out of sync.
They have perfect reactions and no sense of fun, so the absolute block numbers mean nothing. Use this to COMPARE playstyles under identical settings, never to calibrate difficulty. Change a slider below, re-run, and watch which column moves.

⏱ CUT OFF counts runs that hit the 15-minute time cap instead of dying. Any number above 0 there means that policy's row UNDERSTATES it — read the result as "at least this far." Measurement first, conclusions second.

🔮 RUN CAMPAIGN gives each bot a CAREER instead of a snapshot. It starts from zero sigils, runs, earns, spends down its own buy order, and runs again — so it answers the one question a snapshot never can: does carving sigils actually let you go deeper? EARLY vs LATE is the median block of the first and last third of the career; GAIN is the whole bet.
⚠️ Noise floor at n=12 is about ±1 block — require a gain of 2+ before believing it. Put 40–100 in the runs box for a long career: more runs makes GAIN trustworthy and reveals where progression stops.

📈 The sparkline under each policy name is the career curve. A line that rises and then flattens is a tree that ran out of upgrades. PLATEAU is the run number of the last new record — green means it was still improving at the end, orange means it stopped early and the rest of the career was flat.

RUNE RETURN is the number that matters now. It's coin earned from rune kills ÷ coin spent on those runes. Above 1.0× means spending runes is paying for itself — below it, you're bleeding. The old "coin per rune" was lying to us: it just measured how rarely you fired.

🩹 MEND vs 🧪 POTION — Mend heals you on the spot at The Door (wallet-limited). Potions are the field reserve you carry out, capped, and drinking one roots you.

Roster shows up sooner: Mob @2, Pistol Cop @5, Riot Shield @7, Shotgunner @9 — because deaths cluster around blocks 8–11 and a unit that debuts at 10 is a unit nobody has actually fought.

Payout tracks threat: thugs and runners are deliberately sub-1× rune return — hex food. The scary ones pay for their runes. Watch back-hits now that shields actually show up.
